# Decision — v0.1 design completion and implementation delegation

**Decision ID:** BOOKOS-DEC-0002  
**Date:** 2026-08-22  
**Status:** ACCEPTED

## Context

Owner instructed Central Brain to complete BOOK OS v0.1 design at a world-class standard without pausing for approval of every technical detail because implementation time is constrained. The previously accepted product principles and Owner/Central Brain/Codex role split remain in force.

## Decision

Central Brain is authorized to finalize v0.1 technical/editorial design and issue bounded Codex implementation tasks without separate Owner confirmation for each internal technical choice, provided the choice does not alter an accepted product principle or trigger a stop condition in `IMPLEMENTATION_ROADMAP_v0.1.md`.

Owner confirmation remains required for major product intent, creative authority, significant recurring cost/risk, regional-access weakening, public exposure of private data, or other explicitly listed stop conditions.

`CORE_ONTOLOGY.md` v0.1 is accepted as the ontology baseline for implementation, subject only to bounded corrections that preserve its accepted invariants.

## Consequences

- design work proceeds to an implementation-ready baseline;
- Codex receives only bounded tasks against exact authority/HEAD;
- repository documents, not chat, remain continuity authority;
- later refinements use explicit versioning/decision records.
