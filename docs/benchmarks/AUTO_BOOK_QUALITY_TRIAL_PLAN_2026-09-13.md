## Question

Do Auto routing and the complete editorial loop produce materially better accepted nonfiction text
than a single-pass draft, without paying for Astra High/Extra High on routine operations?

## Corpus and privacy

Use two new, non-confidential chapter briefs (one stable conceptual topic and one Russia-specific
fast-changing topic). The supplied «Как продавать услуги» files are quality references for human
judgment only; their text is not copied into prompts or committed.

## Bounded run

- Candidate A: one controlled single-pass manual baseline.
- Candidate B: the full Auto Book research → Writer → Editor → independent critic → correction path.
- Maximum: 2 briefs × 2 candidates, plus one independent blinded evaluation per pair.
- Hard maximum request count: 10 provider requests total.
- Proposed hard total authorization: **USD 5.00**, with the app's current preflight pricing required
  to fit every request before execution; otherwise reduce output size or stop.
- No request may exceed **USD 0.75**. Unknown outcome reserves its full cap and is not retried.
- Stop after the first pair if the remaining reserved budget cannot cover the second pair and judge.

The Owner must separately approve the exact model IDs, token bounds and current price calculation
shown by preflight. This document alone does not authorize calls.

## Blind evaluation

Hide model/routing identity. A reviewer scores promise fulfilment, causal explanation, evidence and
freshness, practical usefulness, whole-text coherence, natural Russian, non-repetition, voice and
cost per accepted 10,000 characters. BLOCKING factual or authority defects make a candidate fail
regardless of its average score.

## Decision

- `GO`: Auto wins or ties quality, clears every blocker, and uses expensive effort selectively.
- `CONDITIONAL_GO`: quality is promising but one named gate/routing rule requires correction.
- `NO_GO`: lower quality, unsupported claims, repeated paid work, or cost above the hard cap.

Record exact prompts, hashes, model/effort provenance, provider run IDs, usage, confirmed cost,
review sheets and accepted revision hashes. Green program tests remain necessary but are not
substitutes for this result.
