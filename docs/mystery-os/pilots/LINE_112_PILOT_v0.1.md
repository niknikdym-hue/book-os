# MYSTERY OS PILOT — «Линия 112» v0.1

**Status:** PILOT PROFILE / STORY NOT YET APPROVED  
**Date:** 2026-09-17

## 1. Role of the pilot

«Линия 112» is the first intended real-book/series pilot for MYSTERY OS.

The pilot exists to test the reusable system under demanding conditions. It must not cause generic MYSTERY OS code or canon to become hard-coded around one series.

## 2. Accepted project direction

Working series title: **«Линия 112»**.

Commercial direction:

- contemporary mystery / crime;
- genuine supernatural layer is allowed and expected, but must remain causally disciplined;
- strong suspense/thriller energy without turning the project into pure horror;
- recurring-series potential;
- text + audiobook suitability;
- entertainment first: the book must be gripping, clear and emotionally involving rather than an exercise in puzzle mechanics.

## 3. Negative brief — explicit rejection of stale default mysticism

Not accepted as default identity:

- archival investigation as the main engine;
- dusty documents, ancestral diaries and old letters carrying the story;
- inherited mansion/old house as automatic mystery source;
- secret room as the main hook;
- generic cursed antiques;
- local-history lecture as suspense substitute;
- pseudo-gothic décor copied from haunted-house fiction;
- fixed “dead people call emergency services” gimmick repeated book after book;
- generic ghost-hunter team;
- chosen-one bloodline reveal;
- occult symbols/rituals inserted only to make scenes look mystical.

The binding anti-cliche policy applies in full.

## 4. Contemporary-world rule

The series should feel recognizably current.

Potentially relevant realities may include, when useful and researched:

- emergency response;
- mobile communications;
- cameras and access systems;
- messaging/social media;
- transport and urban infrastructure;
- hospitals and public services;
- contemporary work/family/social life;
- digital traces and their limitations.

Technology must not become either magic or an author convenience. If a phone, camera, log or service matters to the case, its capabilities and failures must be plausible or explicitly fictionalized.

## 5. The title does not yet define the literal engine

«Линия 112» is accepted as a working series brand.

It does **not** yet establish that:

- the protagonist is necessarily a 112 dispatcher;
- every case begins with a 112 call;
- every supernatural event uses a telephone;
- emergency services are the sole investigative institution;
- the same impossible-call gimmick repeats across books.

Those are future Series Bible / StoryDefinition decisions requiring human approval and originality/commercial testing.

## 6. What the pilot must prove about MYSTERY OS

The pilot should exercise at least:

1. StoryDefinition approval;
2. complete CaseSolution before drafting;
3. suspect and secret architecture;
4. clue lifecycle and fair-play checks;
5. objective CaseTimeline vs narrative disclosure order;
6. character knowledge-state validation;
7. supernatural-rule consistency;
8. anti-cliche gate at concept/architecture/draft/final stages;
9. scene-level writing admission;
10. mandatory mid-book audit;
11. independent adversarial case reconstruction;
12. ColdReader checkpoints;
13. series Book Passport / uniqueness map;
14. whole-book style/AI-prose pathology review;
15. audio-readiness review;
16. exact Literary Master binding.

## 7. Commercial acceptance questions before first-book GO

Before selecting the first plot, MYSTERY OS must produce evidence/diagnosis for:

- likely LitRes/adjacent target reader;
- exact shelf/category promise;
- current comparable successful series and their reader promise;
- what is crowded/overused in that market;
- what «Линия 112» offers that is specific and easy to explain;
- whether the recurring engine can generate several materially different books;
- whether book 1 is strong enough without series familiarity;
- whether title, cover direction and annotation can sell the genre quickly;
- whether audio strengthens the product.

Visible ratings/reviews may be used as engagement signals only, not represented as actual sales data unless sales data is available.

## 8. First-book concept gate

Book 1 must not proceed to architecture until there are several genuinely different concept candidates and the selected concept passes:

- commercial promise;
- case originality;
- character engine;
- supernatural originality/discipline;
- anti-cliche scan;
- series repeatability without template repetition;
- audio potential;
- human Owner choice.

“Genuinely different” means different causal engines, not the same plot with another victim/location.

## 9. Series engine gate

Before systematic drafting, define why recurring protagonists plausibly encounter/investigate cases.

The engine must avoid:

- coincidence-driven repeated body discovery;
- unlimited confidential access without role-based cause;
- authorities becoming stupid so protagonists can matter;
- one supernatural gimmick forced into every volume;
- escalation requiring each next villain to be larger/more grotesque.

## 10. Character requirement

Recurring characters must be worth returning to independently of each case.

They require:

- distinct competence and limits;
- specific voices;
- conflicts arising from values/goals rather than manufactured misunderstanding;
- long arcs with movement across books;
- no stock “damaged genius investigator” package unless radically transformed and human-approved;
- no romance obligation simply because many series include it.

## 11. Supernatural requirement

For the pilot:

- supernatural reality may be genuine;
- it cannot function as an unlimited oracle;
- it cannot replace detection;
- any decisive supernatural capability must be governed by a locked rule before it solves/excludes a hypothesis;
- recurring supernatural ontology must not force identical plots;
- ambiguity may remain where deliberately designed, but the crime/case resolution must still satisfy the selected mystery profile.

## 12. Series-book independence

Default intended model:

- each book resolves its main case;
- recurring-character and series-level arcs may continue;
- later books remain accessible to a new reader with bounded orientation;
- the series rewards order without making book 3 incomprehensible without books 1–2.

This is a pilot hypothesis, subject to concept/market review before lock.

## 13. Content and tone

Target is suspenseful and adult without relying on gratuitous cruelty as a commercial shortcut.

Violence, sexuality, trauma and supernatural fear should serve case/character stakes. “Darker” is not automatically “better”.

## 14. Agents API/Codex pre-writing use

The pilot may optionally use the approved Agents API/Codex lane for **pre-writing preparation** when the Owner chooses to run it.

Permitted preparation outputs include proposals for:

- market/comparable analysis;
- several first-book concepts;
- series-engine alternatives;
- recurring-character candidates;
- StoryDefinition draft;
- CaseSolution candidate;
- suspect/secret matrix;
- clue and red-herring map;
- objective CaseTimeline;
- MysticRuleSet candidate;
- RevealPlan;
- anti-cliche/adversarial critique;
- writing-readiness packet.

All such outputs remain `PROPOSED`. They do not unlock Writing until human gates in MYSTERY OS are satisfied.

The preparation agent must not hard-code one fashionable gimmick merely because the title is «Линия 112».

## 15. Pilot success criteria

MYSTERY OS pilot is not successful merely because a complete manuscript exists.

Success requires evidence that:

- the solved case remained coherent through drafting/editing;
- fair-play and timeline tools found/prevented real defects;
- anti-cliche layer prevented generic solutions or caught them early;
- recurring characters support future demand;
- book-level and series-level originality survived comparison;
- ColdReader/adversarial review finds the reveal earned;
- human editorial judgment considers the manuscript professionally publishable after required revision;
- system overhead is justified by quality improvement;
- reusable logic can be promoted without hard-coding «Линия 112» into BOOK OS.

## 16. Current state

Accepted now:

- project/series working title: `Линия 112`;
- broad genre direction: contemporary mystical mystery/detective/thriller;
- commercial priority;
- strong rejection of stale archive/haunted-house defaults;
- MYSTERY OS-first process before story writing;
- optional Agents API/Codex use for bounded pre-writing preparation.
